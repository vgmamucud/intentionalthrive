You see a doctor once a year for your body. Your finances deserve the same, and it takes an hour.

Block time in December or on your birthday, pour a coffee, and answer these ten questions.

## Cash

1. **Is my emergency fund where I want it?** One month, three, six?
2. **What did I spend on that I wouldn't choose again?** Scan your statements for the pattern.

## Protection

3. **Would my income be protected if I couldn't work?** Check life, health and critical illness cover against your current life.
4. **Are my beneficiaries correct?** Anyone to add or remove?
5. **Are any premiums at risk of lapsing?** Confirm due dates and payment methods.

## Debt

6. **What do I owe, and what does it cost?** Are the highest-cost balances shrinking?

## Growth

7. **Am I investing regularly, and is it suited to my timeline?** Review fund fact sheets, not just balances.
8. **What is my net worth compared with last year?** Direction matters more than size.

## Life

9. **What changed this year?** Job, family, health, goals. Does my plan still fit?
10. **What's the one thing to do next?** Pick one action, with a date.

## How to run it

- Gather statements, policies and account balances beforehand.
- Write answers in one document, kept with your important papers.
- Schedule any follow-up calls or changes while the list is fresh.
- Share one line with your partner or a trusted person.

## What you'll likely find

Usually small things: a lapsed beneficiary, a forgotten subscription, a goal that needs a new number. Fixing them early is easy. Finding them in a crisis isn't.

Put the date in your calendar before you close this page.
