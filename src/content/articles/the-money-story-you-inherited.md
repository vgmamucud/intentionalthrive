Most of us didn't choose our relationship with money. We absorbed it.

It came in through overheard arguments, through what was celebrated and what was whispered, through the way an envelope was handed over or withheld. By the time you earned your first salary, you already had a set of beliefs. You just never wrote them down.

## The sentences that run your decisions

Try finishing these without overthinking:

- "Money is…"
- "People who have a lot of money are…"
- "Talking about money is…"
- "If I ask for more, I'm…"

Your answers are rarely your own opinions. They're usually someone else's, repeated so often they sound like facts.

Some common inherited stories in Filipino households:

- **"Don't show that you have money."** Which can turn into never planning openly for it.
- **"Family comes first, always."** Which can quietly mean you come last.
- **"Insurance is for when you're old or sick."** Which delays protection until it's costly or unavailable.
- **"Debt is shameful."** Which hides debts instead of solving them.
- **"We're not good with money."** Which becomes a prophecy.

None of these came from bad intentions. Most came from people doing their best with scarcity.

## Why it matters

A story you can't see runs your choices automatically. You undercharge, overgive, avoid the statement, or never ask what you own, and wonder why discipline isn't enough.

## Rewriting without rejecting

You don't have to disown where you come from. The goal isn't to decide your family was wrong. It's to separate what served them from what serves you.

Ask of each belief:

1. **Where did I learn this?**
2. **What was it protecting against?**
3. **Is that threat still true for me?**
4. **What would I choose if I were deciding fresh?**

Sometimes the old belief still holds, and you keep it on purpose. Sometimes you update it, and that's growth.

## A small exercise

Take ten minutes tonight. Write the four sentences above. Next to each, write who you first heard it from. Then write one version you'd like to hold instead.

Don't change your budget yet. Just see the script.

Understanding comes first. Decisions made after you know your story are very different from decisions made inside it.
